## Identity Confirmation

The email correspondent abbreviated as **"ader"** throughout the DOJ EFTA datasets is **Ariane de Rothschild**, CEO of Edmond de Rothschild Holding S.A. The abbreviation derives from **A**riane **de** **R**othschild. This identification is confirmed by:

- Correspondence topics (DOJ Swiss Bank Program, Luxembourg regulator, internal Rothschild banking operations) matching her known role
- The $25M contract (October 5, 2015) signed by Ariane de Rothschild connecting directly to wire discussions in the emails
- References to "benj" (Benjamin de Rothschild, her late husband and then-chairman), "David" (David de Rothschild, rival branch head), and Luxembourg regulatory matters consistent with Edmond de Rothschild Group operations

## Revised Analytical Summary

### Epstein's True Role in the Rothschild Relationship

The prior characterization — Epstein as a financial consultant paid $25M for "risk analysis" — is significantly incomplete. The email record establishes the following functions:

**1. DOJ Intermediary (2014–2015)**
From at least June 2014, Epstein was actively coordinating with Ariane to manage Edmond de Rothschild Group's document submissions to the DOJ under the Swiss Bank Program. He was receiving real-time intelligence on DOJ document demands ("Yves told me that DOJ asked for more documents") and was alarmed when independent DOJ counsel (Steptoe) was being engaged — suggesting he expected to remain the primary channel.

**2. Financial Structuring Advisor**
The December 15, 2015 email discussing "separating payments from Lugano and Geneva" and the tax treatment of the DOJ penalty is pre-payment financial structuring advice. The $25M was not a simple consulting fee — it appears to be payment structured in coordination with Epstein for his DOJ crisis management role, the terms of which were openly discussed in email.

**3. "Cleaning the Bank"**
Epstein's December 2017 comment that "cleaning the bank was brilliant" — following the June 2016 police raid — suggests the "restructuring" he referenced in July 2014 involved deliberate preparation of the bank's account/documentation profile for regulatory scrutiny.

**4. Network Broker**
Epstein brokered Ariane's access to Prince Andrew, offered to negotiate with David de Rothschild on her behalf, arranged introductions to figures such as "Leonard Fisher," and served as her primary confidant in internal Rothschild power conflicts.

**5. Ongoing Strategic Advisor (2017–2019)**
Post-payment, Epstein continued advising on bank strategy (merger/acquisition scenarios), Luxembourg regulatory matters, and acting as confidential communications channel ("please avoid these emails" — suggesting a more secure channel existed).
