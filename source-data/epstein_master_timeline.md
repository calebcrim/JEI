## Era 1: Pre-History & Early Network (Before 1990)

### ~1953-01-20 — Jeffrey Epstein Born
**People:** Jeffrey Epstein
**Source:** epstein_themes_synthesis.md, epstein_research.html

Jeffrey Edward Epstein born January 20, 1953, in Brooklyn, New York. His parents were working-class Jewish immigrants. He attended Lafayette High School in Brooklyn.

### ~1974 — Epstein Hired at Dalton School
**People:** Jeffrey Epstein
**Source:** epstein_themes_synthesis.md

Epstein, who did not complete a college degree, was hired as a math and physics teacher at the Dalton School in Manhattan. He met investment banker Alan "Ace" Greenberg's son there, creating the social connection that would launch his finance career.

### ~1976 — Epstein Joins Bear Stearns
**People:** Jeffrey Epstein
**Source:** epstein_themes_synthesis.md

Through the Greenberg connection, Epstein was hired at Bear Stearns, eventually rising to limited partner by 1980. He left under disputed circumstances circa 1981 — officially for violating SEC regulations regarding client funds.

**People:** Jeffrey Epstein, Leslie Wexner
**Source:** epstein_themes_synthesis.md

After leaving Bear Stearns, Epstein founded J. Epstein & Co., presenting himself as an exclusive financial advisor who would only accept clients with $1 billion or more in assets. The claimed strategy involved sophisticated tax and asset protection structures. In reality, his primary client was Wexner.

## Era 3: Investigation, Plea Deal & Coverup (2003–2009)

### 2005 — Palm Beach Police Investigation Opens
**People:** Michael Reiter (Police Chief), Jeffrey Epstein, 27+ victims
**Source:** Multiple

Palm Beach Police Department opened an investigation after a parent reported that Epstein had paid her 14-year-old daughter for massages. Chief Michael Reiter led the investigation.
