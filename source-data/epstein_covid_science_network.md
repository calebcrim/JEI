# Epstein's Science Network & Pandemic Expertise: Documented Connections, Missing COVID Links, and Institutional Corruption

> **Research methodology note:** This report separates findings into three tiers:
> - 🟢 **DOCUMENTED** — court filings, Bates-stamped DOJ releases, financial records, flight logs
> - 🟡 **REPORTED** — credible journalism with named sources
> - 🔴 **SPECULATIVE** — forum claims, unverified allegations, extrapolated connections

**Bottom line up front:** The Epstein files reveal a man who systematically infiltrated elite scientific networks at Harvard and MIT, concentrating on genetics, evolutionary biology, and transhumanism. Three scientists in his documented orbit — Nathan Wolfe (virology/pandemic preparedness), George Church (synthetic biology), and Martin Nowak (virus dynamics) — possess expertise directly relevant to pandemic science. However, after exhaustive review of the 3.5 million pages released by the DOJ under the [Epstein Files Transparency Act](https://www.justice.gov/epstein), **no evidence connects Epstein to COVID-19 research, gain-of-function work, the Wuhan Institute of Virology, or operational pandemic planning**.

## 1. Nathan Wolfe — The Most Direct Pandemic-to-Epstein Link

🟢 **DOCUMENTED**

[Nathan Wolfe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Wolfe) — Stanford virologist, pandemic preparedness expert, and author of *The Viral Storm: The Dawn of a New Pandemic Age* (2011) — is **mentioned 589 times** in DOJ-released documents, making him the most pandemic-relevant figure in the entire Epstein archive.

Wolfe's pandemic credentials are extensive:
- Founded the [Global Viral Forecasting Initiative](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Nathan-Wolfe) (2007)
- Founded **Metabiota**, a biological threat evaluation company later linked to DoD-funded Ukraine biolab monitoring
- Served on DARPA's Defense Science Research Council
- Sat on the editorial board of *EcoHealth*, the journal of EcoHealth Alliance (which funded coronavirus research at the Wuhan Institute of Virology)

DOJ emails (Bates ref: **EFTA00961409**) show Wolfe corresponded with Epstein from 2008 to 2013, visited his homes in New York and Florida, and proposed Epstein fund a study of undergraduate sexual behavior to test what they called the "horny virus hypothesis" — investigating microbial influences on human sexual activity.

In December 2010, Wolfe invited Epstein to a dinner party, describing attendees as including "a couple of hottie interns from WEF." In email sign-offs, Wolfe urged Epstein to "never let the bastards get you down."

Following revelations in the 2026 DOJ releases, the [Stanford Daily reported](https://stanforddaily.com/2026/02/03/former-stanford-professor-nathan-wolfe-92-planned-sexual-behavior-research-described-interns-with-epstein/) that Wolfe planned the sexual behavior research with Epstein and described interns to him. Wolfe stated he never received Epstein funding, never visited his island, and never flew on his plane. Stanford subsequently [ended his visiting appointment](https://stanforddaily.com/2026/02/16/nathan-wolfe-end-stanford-epstein/).

**The indirect Metabiota–EcoHealth link:** Wolfe's professional overlap with EcoHealth Alliance through the PREDICT pandemic surveillance program creates a network bridge — but it is a professional association, not a documented Epstein→EcoHealth connection. Claims of a direct Epstein–Peter Daszak link extrapolate through Wolfe without documentary support.

## 2. Martin Nowak — Virus Dynamics & $6.5 Million in Harvard Funding

🟢 **DOCUMENTED**

[Martin Nowak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Nowak) is the author of *[Virus Dynamics: Mathematical Principles of Immunology and Virology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Nowak)* (2000) — a foundational text that established core concepts including the basic reproductive ratio (R₀) now ubiquitous in COVID-19 epidemiology.

Epstein gave Nowak **$6.5 million** to establish Harvard's Program for Evolutionary Dynamics (PED) in 2003 — the single largest Epstein science gift. Nowak's name appears over **4,000 times** in DOJ documents.

Key documented facts from DOJ files:
- Epstein maintained a personal office ("Jeffrey's Office," Room 610) in the PED building
- Visited PED over 40 times between 2010–2018, holding an unrestricted keycard
- Hosted meetings with scientists including George Church and Noam Chomsky
- Reviewed Nowak's *Nature* paper page proofs before publication
- Suggested research topics including "commercial evolution" and "prelife"
- A 2014 email (Bates **EFTA00748457**): Nowak wrote "our spy was captured after completing her mission"; Epstein replied "did you torture her"

Harvard shuttered PED in 2021. [Nowak was sanctioned in 2021](https://www.harvardmagazine.com/2021/03/martin-nowak-sanctioned-for-jeffrey-epstein-involvement), sanctions [were lifted in March 2023](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2023/5/22/nowak-sanctions-lifted/), then he was [placed on administrative leave again in February 2026](https://www.harvardmagazine.com/university-news/martin-nowak-harvard-administrative-leave-epstein) following new revelations in the DOJ file dumps.

## 3. George Church — Genetics, Synthetic Biology & Epstein's Tissue Samples

🟢 **DOCUMENTED**

[George Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Church_(geneticist)) — Harvard geneticist, synthetic biology pioneer, and a founder of the field of genomics — received **$686,000** directly from the Jeffrey Epstein VI Foundation (2005–2007) plus approximately $2 million through donors Epstein brokered.

The two co-founded an investment company called **"Georgarage"** in 2014 focused on genetics startups, with Epstein proposing $10 million in funding. In 2013, Epstein enrolled in Church's **Personal Genome Project**, causing an internal furor when a staff member attempted to fast-track sequencing his biological sample. The [Boston Globe reported in February 2026](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/02/24/business/jeffrey-epstein-george-church-harvard-tissue-samples-lab/) that a researcher threatened to quit after discovering Epstein's identity. [STAT News covered the same story](https://www.statnews.com/2026/02/24/epstein-cell-line-george-church-harvard-personal-genome-project/).

**COVID-19 adjacency:** After Epstein's death, Church co-founded **[RaDVaC (Rapid Deployment Vaccine Collaborative)](https://radvac.org/about/)** in 2020 — a DIY nasal COVID-19 vaccine initiative. His lab also developed an mRNA vaccine platform now licensed by Harvard's Office of Technology Development. These projects postdate the Epstein relationship by years and are not connected to it.

🟡 **REPORTED — CNN (Feb 6, 2026):** Epstein paid [Harvard geneticist Joseph Thakuria](https://www.cnn.com/2026/02/06/politics/jeffrey-epstein-genetic-test-joseph-thakuria-harvard) for novel genetic testing and proposed CRISPR gene-editing of his own stem cells "to introduce mutations believed to increase longevity," with invoices totaling $160,000+ for something called "The Venus Project."

## 4. Boris Nikolic — Epstein's Will, Pandemic Emails & Biotech Investing

🟢 **DOCUMENTED**

[Boris Nikolic](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-08-19/epstein-s-11th-hour-executor-is-ex-science-adviser-to-bill-gates) — a Harvard-trained immunologist who served as chief science and technology adviser to the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation — was named **successor executor** in Epstein's will, signed just two days before Epstein's death in August 2019. [Bloomberg broke the story](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/epstein-s-11th-hour-executor-is-ex-science-adviser-to-bill-gates-1.1303888); Nikolic said he was "shocked" and [declined the role](https://www.foxbusiness.com/features/who-is-boris-nikolic-epstein-executor).

Nikolic co-founded **Biomatics Capital**, a biotech venture firm that invested in gene-editing companies including eGenesis (co-founded by George Church) and Editas Medicine.

**The pandemic preparedness emails — three key documents:**

1. **2011:** Epstein advised JPMorgan's Jes Staley on a Gates-linked donor-advised fund, mentioning "additional money for vaccines" and an "offshore arm — especially for vaccines."

2. **March 2015:** An email forwarded to Epstein contained a "draft agenda for the meeting on preparing for pandemics" discussing WHO and ICRC involvement in a Geneva gathering (cited in DOJ Data Set 11).

3. **May 2017:** Nikolic wrote in an email to Gates and Epstein that donor-advised funds "might be a great path forward for some key areas such as Energy, **pandemic**, etc." — three years before COVID-19.

4. **2017 — bgC3 document (Bates EFTA02657725):** A document from Gates' private office listed "Strain pandemic simulation" among deliverables. [Tempo fact-checked](https://en.tempo.co/read/2086521/fact-check-claims-of-bill-gates-planning-covid-19-in-epstein-files-misleading) claims connecting this to COVID-19 planning as **misleading**. [Fact Crescendo similarly found](https://srilanka.factcrescendo.com/english/no-evidence-epstein-files-show-bill-gates-discussed-strain-pandemic-simulation-with-jeffrey-epstein/) no evidence of coordinated COVID planning. Gates called the claim "false" in February 2026.

🟡 **REPORTED — Children's Health Defense analysis:** The CHD, which is sympathetic to pandemic-planning theories, [reviewed the Epstein files](https://childrenshealthdefense.org/defender/epstein-pandemic-strategies-involvement-what-the-emails-do-dont-tell-us/) and concluded: "There is no document in the reviewed Epstein materials that demonstrates coordination of COVID-19, shows operational influence over WHO pandemic declarations, or links Epstein directly to vaccine platform development."

## 7. Scientists NOT Connected to Epstein (COVID Origins Figures)

🟢 **DOCUMENTED — absence of evidence**

Despite extensive searching across DOJ releases, flight logs, the Epstein black book, financial records, and credible journalism, **no documented connection exists** between Epstein and the following figures central to COVID-19 research and response:

- **Peter Daszak** (EcoHealth Alliance): No meetings, funding, flights, or correspondence found in any document or credible reporting. The only indirect link is Wolfe's professional overlap with EcoHealth through the PREDICT program. Claims of a direct connection originate from conspiracy-oriented outlets extrapolating through Wolfe.

- **Ralph Baric** (UNC coronavirus researcher): No evidence of any connection.

- **Anthony Fauci / NIAID**: No documented relationship. Viral claims linking Fauci to Epstein through Moderna have been [debunked by PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2020/aug/13/facebook-posts/conspiracy-about-moderna-gates-epstein-fauci-and-s/) and are rated **false**. Fauci was never Moderna's CEO; Epstein was never a Moderna stockholder.

- **Kristian Andersen** (Scripps Research): No evidence whatsoever.

- **Wuhan Institute of Virology scientists**: No connection. The frequently cited **Charles Lieber** case (Harvard chemistry professor [arrested January 2020](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/peteraldhous/eric-lander-biden-science-adviser) for concealing work at Wuhan University of Technology) involves a *different* Wuhan institution, and Lieber received no documented Epstein funding — the connection is solely that both were at Harvard.

- **Eric Lander** (Broad Institute co-founder, Biden science adviser): [Met Epstein twice in 2012](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/peteraldhous/eric-lander-biden-science-adviser); his confirmation as science adviser was delayed due to the meetings. He later [resigned for unrelated bullying conduct](https://www.science.org/content/article/white-house-science-adviser-eric-lander-under-fire-bullying). His pandemic preparedness work as OSTP director postdated and was unrelated to Epstein contact.

## 9. Epstein's Actual Biology Obsession: Eugenics & Transhumanism

🟢 **DOCUMENTED**

Epstein's documented interest in biological sciences was overwhelmingly focused on **genetics, eugenics, transhumanism, and longevity** — not infectious disease or biodefense.

- [New York Times reported (2019)](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/2634852/epstein-wanted-to-seed-humanity-with-his-genes/) Epstein planned to "seed the human race with his DNA" by impregnating 20 women at his New Mexico ranch

This eugenics fixation drove his relationships with Church (CRISPR), Nowak (evolutionary dynamics), and Thakuria (longevity genetics). **No credible source documents Epstein expressing interest in biological weapons, pathogen engineering, or deliberate pandemic planning.**
