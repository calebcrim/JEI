## Category 1: The Principal — Jeffrey Epstein

### Jeffrey Epstein
**Born:** January 20, 1953, Brooklyn, New York
**Died:** August 10, 2019, Metropolitan Correctional Center, Manhattan (ruled suicide; contested)
**Category:** Central figure
**Source files:** All 9 source files

**Background and early career:** Epstein attended Lafayette High School in Brooklyn and enrolled at Cooper Union and then New York University, though he did not complete a degree. In 1974 he was hired as a math teacher at the Dalton School in Manhattan, where he met investment banker Alan "Ace" Greenberg's son. This connection led to a position at Bear Stearns circa 1976, where he rose to limited partner by 1980. He left Bear Stearns under disputed circumstances in 1981 — officially for violating SEC regulations — and founded J. Epstein & Co., ostensibly a private financial advisory firm serving billionaires with a minimum client threshold of $1 billion in assets. His claimed expertise was tax and asset strategies for ultra-high-net-worth clients.

**Relationship with Wexner:** The foundational relationship of Epstein's wealth was with Limited Brands founder Leslie Wexner, who hired Epstein as his money manager in the mid-1980s. In 1991, Wexner granted Epstein a sweeping power of attorney giving him "full power and authority to do and perform every act necessary" over finances, investments, and property. Wexner transferred the 9 East 71st Street Manhattan townhouse (purchased for $13.2 million in 1989) to Epstein for $0, along with the Palm Beach estate. House Oversight Democrats stated the total transferred was approximately **$1 billion**; Wexner's attorneys told investigators Epstein had repaid $100 million — "thought to be just a portion of what he stole." [HO, DOJ]

**Properties:** Epstein owned or controlled: the Manhattan townhouse at 9 East 71st Street (the largest private residence in Manhattan by some accounts); a Palm Beach mansion at 358 El Brillo Way; Little St. James Island (USVI, ~70 acres, "Lolita Express" destination); Zorro Ranch, New Mexico (~7,600–10,000 acres, purchased 1993 from Gov. Bruce King family for ~$12 million through shell company "Zorro Trust"); and a Paris apartment on Avenue Foch. He also controlled an apartment at 301 East 66th Street, Manhattan (technically owned by brother Mark Epstein), which staff referred to as "Ehud's apartment." [Multiple sources]

**Financial structure:** Epstein operated through an elaborate web of shell entities including Southern Trust Company Inc. (USVI, $391.3 million in assets by 2017, 13 employees, claimed to provide "biomedical and financial informatics" — actually a payment conduit), Financial Trust Company Inc., Plan D LLC (holding the Boeing 727 and Gulfstream G550), Hyperion Air LLC (helicopters), Gratitude America Ltd. (philanthropy front), The 1953 Trust (final estate vehicle, $577–600 million), Butterfly Trust, La Hougue, HBRK Associates, Coatue Enterprises, C.O.U.Q. Foundation, Enhanced Education, JEGE Inc./LLC, NES LLC, Georgarage LLC, and others.

**The trafficking operation:** The core criminal enterprise involved recruiting underage girls under the guise of massage therapy, sexually abusing them at his multiple properties, and sharing them with associates. Recruiters were paid $200 per girl they brought. Epstein told recruiter Haley Robson "the younger the better." The DOJ files documented a recruitment pyramid: Palm Beach Police identified 27 girls and women aged 14–23, FBI grand jury testimony confirmed "more than a majority would have been under the age of 18," and the 86-page SDNY co-conspirators memo (December 19, 2019) contained statements from 24 women abused as minors and 14 abused as adults. A 32-count draft indictment named 19 victims (Jane Does #1–#19), the youngest 14 years old. [CBS, HO, DOJ]

**Emails and communications:** Epstein's emails (accessible via JMail at jmail.world, cataloging 1,038,603 emails from jeevacation@gmail.com and jeeproject@yahoo.com) reveal extensive communications with Bannon, Gates, Summers, Wolff, Clinton, Chomsky, and dozens of others. Key emails include: April 2, 2011 to Maxwell ("that dog that hasn't barked is trump"); January 31, 2019 to Wolff ("Of course he knew about the girls as he asked ghislaine to stop"); a December 2015 email to NYT reporter Landon Thomas Jr. offering "photos of donald and girls in bikinis in my kitchen"; a 2018 email about Trump: "I am the one able to take him down"; a 2017 email to Summers calling Trump "the most dangerous person I have ever met." Epstein also communicated detailed transhumanist ambitions: plans to impregnate 20 women simultaneously at Zorro Ranch; a 2018 designer-baby proposal to Bryan Bishop for $1.7M/year; a 2016 email to Joscha Bach about genetically modifying Black people. [JMail, HO, OSINT]

**Austrian passport:** Dataset 8 released an expired Austrian passport under the alias **"Marius Robert Fortelni"** listing a Saudi Arabian address. [DOJ]

**Final days:** On August 8, 2019, Epstein signed the **1953 Trust** will ($577.6 million estate), naming Darren Indyke and Richard Kahn as executors, with beneficiaries including $100M to Karyna Shuliak, $50M to Indyke, $25M to Kahn, $10M each to Maxwell and brother Mark Epstein, and $5M to Harvard professor Martin Nowak. On August 9, his cellmate Efrain Reyes was transferred out; that evening he made an unmonitored phone call (telling staff he was calling his "mother," who died in 2003 — actually calling girlfriend Karyna Shuliak). On August 10, 2019, he was found dead at approximately 6:30 AM, suspended from the top bunk by a makeshift noose. Official ruling: suicide by hanging. Contested by Dr. Michael Baden and Epstein's family. [CBS, OSINT]

**Intelligence connections:** EFTA00090314 contains an FBI Confidential Human Source report (unverified) claiming Epstein "was a co-opted Mossad agent trained as a spy" for Israel, and that Dershowitz told then-US Attorney Acosta that Epstein "belonged to both U.S. and allied intelligence services." A 2018 email about Robert Maxwell claims Maxwell threatened Mossad: "unless they gave him £400 million to save his crumbling empire, he would expose all he had done for them." Acosta reportedly told Trump transition vetting team he was told Epstein "belonged to intelligence" with a "leave-it-alone" status (denied by Acosta under oath). **[UNVERIFIED — single-source claims]** [DOJ, OSINT]

## Category 2: Operational Inner Circle

### Ghislaine Maxwell
**Born:** December 25, 1961, Maisons-Laffitte, France
**Category:** Co-conspirator / operational director
**Current status:** Convicted; serving 20 years at FCI Tallahassee
**Source files:** All 9 source files

Maxwell, daughter of British media mogul Robert Maxwell (who died under suspicious circumstances in 1991), became Epstein's primary partner and alleged procurer. She was convicted on December 29, 2021, of five federal charges including sex trafficking of a minor, and sentenced to 20 years on June 28, 2022, by Judge Alison Nathan.

**Operational role:** Maxwell handled the day-to-day management of the trafficking operation, recruited victims, participated in abuse, and coordinated staff. Government Exhibit GX 606 (household manual) instructed staff to "see nothing, hear nothing, say nothing" and "NEVER disclose Mr. Epstein or Ms. Maxwell's activities." Flight records (GX 662) showed Maxwell and Sarah Kellen flew together approximately 39 times in 2003 alone. Maxwell appeared on approximately 400–520 documented flight legs — the most frequent flier after Epstein. Her initials ("GM") appear on each of Clinton's 26 flight legs. [DOJ, CBS, GH]

**Key testimony:** In her April 22, 2016 videotaped deposition (Giuffre v. Maxwell), Maxwell denied most allegations. At her 2025 interview with DAG Todd Blanche, she stated Clinton was "my friend, not Epstein's friend." She confirmed her father Robert Maxwell "functioned as" an intelligence officer "throughout his life, passing on secrets to governments, including Israel." At her February 10, 2026 House Oversight deposition, she invoked the Fifth Amendment throughout; her attorney offered testimony exonerating both Trump and Clinton in exchange for clemency: "Both President Trump and President Clinton are innocent of any wrongdoing. Ms. Maxwell alone can explain why." [CBS, HO, OSINT]

**Topless photos:** Juan Alessi's deposition (Giuffre v. Maxwell, Dkt. 1158) described Maxwell taking topless photos of young women. Maxwell denied providing Giuffre "an outfit of a sexual nature to wear for Les Wexner." An October 2005 email shows Maxwell told Epstein she met a CIA operative who "worked" with her father and could "find all, and reveal all (for a price)." [DOJ, GH]

**Melania email:** October 23, 2002: Melania Trump emailed Maxwell: "Dear G! How are you? Nice story about JE in NY mag. You look great in the picture… Give me a call when you are back in NY." Maxwell replied warmly, calling her "Sweet pea." [CBS, OSINT]

**Three wiretap discs:** The Maxwell grand jury transcripts were ordered released December 9, 2025. Three discs containing wiretap results from Maxwell's phone number have not been publicly released. [DOJ]

**Finances:** Dataset 11 includes Maxwell's UBS account wiring instructions. A June 15, 2007 wire transfer showed $7.4 million moved from Epstein's BNY account to Maxwell's JPMorgan account; Maxwell moved it the same day to "Air Ghislaine, Inc."; three days later $7.3 million went to Sikorsky Aircraft for a helicopter. EFTA01296151 contains a trust agreement naming Maxwell as beneficiary. [DOJ, OSINT]

## Category 3: The NPA and Legal Structure

### Alexander Acosta (Alex Acosta)
**Born:** January 16, 1969
**Category:** Former U.S. Attorney / political figure
**Source files:** All 9 source files

As U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of Florida, Acosta negotiated and signed the Non-Prosecution Agreement (NPA) on September 24, 2007 — one day before AUSA Marie Villafaña was prepared to file the indictment. The NPA granted immunity to Epstein plus four named co-conspirators plus "any potential co-conspirators." The original 53-page draft federal indictment with 60 criminal counts prepared by Villafaña in May 2007 was never filed.

Acosta served as Trump's Labor Secretary from April 2017 until July 2019, when he resigned amid renewed scrutiny of the plea deal following Epstein's arrest. In a September 19, 2025 closed-door interview with the House Oversight Committee (transcript released October 17, 2025), Acosta said a DOJ Child Exploitation section attorney called going to trial a "crapshoot" due to evidentiary issues. He denied ever meeting Epstein or Maxwell and denied seeing Trump's name on any Epstein-related document. He denied under oath the quote attributed to him (by journalist Vicky Ward) that he was told Epstein "belonged to intelligence" and was given a "leave-it-alone" directive. The DOJ Office of Professional Responsibility report found his handling was "poor judgment" but not "professional misconduct." [Multiple sources]
