# The Epstein OSINT Database: a comprehensive sweep across four investigative axes

**The Epstein OSINT Database on Notion is live and actively maintained at `epstein-osint-database.notion.site`, forming one node in an explosive post-EFTA ecosystem of 78+ community tools processing 218 GB of DOJ-released data.** This sweep across the database, its mirrors, and corroborating public sources reveals a deeply documented network of financial, personal, and evidentiary connections — with significant gaps that appear deliberate. The January 30, 2026 DOJ release of 3.5 million pages under the Epstein Files Transparency Act triggered an unprecedented wave of open-source intelligence work, but the DOJ's subsequent removal of Datasets 9–11 and NPR's February 24, 2026 finding of **53 missing pages** of FBI interview documents have intensified scrutiny of what remains withheld.

## The database ecosystem and its architecture

The Epstein OSINT Database is a JavaScript-rendered Notion site organized into structured categories: **People** (with role tags like "Staff" and "Select"), **Organizations**, **Flight Logs**, **Properties**, and **Docket Entries**. Individual entries include biographical summaries, known connections, and source documentation. Indexed entries visible in search results include profiles for financial executives (Gregory Hold of Hold Brothers Capital), compliance analysts (Cashkim Bussue, linked to LSJE LLC), and figures from Epstein's modeling network (Jill Dodd, connected to Jean-Luc Brunel and Adnan Khashoggi).

The Notion database cannot be scraped via standard tools due to its JavaScript rendering, and **no mirror or static export of the curated database itself exists**, unlike the raw DOJ files which are heavily mirrored. Three key GitHub repositories supplement it. The **rhowardstone/Epstein-research** repo contains over 100 forensic analysis reports derived from the 218 GB release, including a 1,536-person entity registry, a knowledge graph of 524 entities and 2,096 connections, and a complete media catalog of all 419 MCC surveillance videos. The **yung-megafone/Epstein-Files** repo (418 stars) maintains magnet links, SHA256 hashes, and mirrors for all 12 DOJ datasets, documenting the community's scramble to preserve Datasets 9–11 after the DOJ removed them in February 2026. The **paulgp/epstein-document-search** repo provides Meilisearch-powered full-text search across court documents.

Beyond these, the community infrastructure includes **JMail.world** (a Gmail-style interface for Epstein emails with 450M+ page views), **epsteinexposed.com** (semantic search across 264K+ documents using pgvector), **epstein-file-explorer.com** (200+ named individuals with AI summaries), and active forums on r/Epstein, r/DataHoarder, r/EpsteinPublicDatasets, and Lemmy instances. A February 13, 2026 HackerNoon article documented OSINT teams using Neo4j, Maltego, and Mermaid.js for network analysis of the files.

## Focus Area 1: Trump-Epstein connections are documented across eight evidence categories

The documentary record linking Trump and Epstein spans flight logs, property connections, witness testimony, emails, communications with associates, and FBI interview records — though Trump has never been charged with any crime connected to Epstein.

**Flight logs confirm at least seven domestic flights** between 1993 and 1997 on Epstein's aircraft, primarily on the Palm Beach–New Jersey/New York corridor. A January 7, 2020 email from an SDNY assistant U.S. attorney stated Trump traveled on Epstein's jet "many more times than previously has been reported," listing him on "at least eight flights between 1993 and 1996" (document EFTA00016732). On one 1993 flight, Trump and Epstein were the only two listed passengers. On another, the three passengers were Epstein, Trump, and a redacted 20-year-old woman. Two flights included women identified as potential witnesses in the Maxwell case. No documented flights to Little St. James Island exist.

**Document EFTA00000468** — the Trump-Epstein-Maxwell photo — became a flashpoint when the DOJ removed it from its website on December 20, 2025, one day after its release. The file showed a desk with framed photographs and an open drawer containing an image of Trump alongside Epstein, Maxwell, and Melania Trump at a February 12, 2000 Mar-a-Lago party. After House Oversight Democrats accused AG Pam Bondi of deletion and Senate Judiciary Democrats asked "What else is being covered up?", the DOJ restored the file without alteration.

Three emails released by House Oversight Democrats reveal Epstein's strategic thinking about Trump. In an April 2, 2011 email to Maxwell, Epstein wrote: **"I want you to realize that that dog that hasn't barked is trump. [Victim 1] spent hours at my house with him,, he has never once been mentioned."** In a January 31, 2019 email to journalist Michael Wolff, Epstein wrote: "Trump said he asked me to resign, never a member ever. Of course he knew about the girls as he asked ghislaine to stop." And in a December 15, 2015 email, Wolff counseled Epstein: "If he says he hasn't been on the plane or to the house, then that gives you a valuable PR and political currency. You can hang him in a way that potentially generates a positive benefit for you."

The Bannon-Epstein communications comprise **hundreds of text messages and emails** between late 2017 and Epstein's July 2019 arrest. Bannon told Epstein he was "the one person I was truly afraid of coming forward during the campaign." Epstein responded: "Now you can understand why trump wakes up in the middle of the night sweating when he hears you and I are friends." Bannon filmed 15+ hours of documentary interviews with Epstein, helped rehabilitate his image ("you are NOT that"), and exchanged Hermes Apple Watches as gifts. His podcast "War Room" stopped discussing Epstein after the files were released.

Property connections are extensive. Epstein's Palm Beach mansion at 360 El Brillo Way sat approximately **2 miles north of Mar-a-Lago**. Epstein was a dues-paying Mar-a-Lago member until October 2007 — more than a year after his 2006 indictment — expelled after reportedly harassing a member's teenage daughter. Trump outbid Epstein to purchase Maison de L'Amitié in 2004 for $41.35 million, a loss Epstein reportedly viewed as a betrayal, and later sold it to Russian oligarch Dmitry Rybolovlev for $95 million. Multiple Trump administration officials appear in the Epstein files, including Alexander Acosta (who led the controversial 2008 plea deal), Howard Lutnick (admitted visiting Epstein's island in 2012), Thomas Barrack (in regular contact with Epstein for years after his conviction), and Steve Bannon.

The "Katie Johnson"/Jane Doe lawsuit — alleging rape by Trump and Epstein in 1994 when the plaintiff was 13 — was filed in April 2016, refiled twice, and voluntarily dismissed on November 4, 2016 after the plaintiff received threats. Attorney Cheney Mason told journalist Tara Palmeri he spent days interrogating the claims and "there's no doubt in my mind she told the truth." Michael Cohen confirmed he was dispatched by Trump to "take care of it." The involvement of Norm Lubow, a former Jerry Springer producer, in promoting the lawsuit raises credibility questions.

## Focus Area 2: $1.5 billion in suspicious transactions across a web of shell entities

The financial architecture surrounding Epstein reveals a systematic pattern: **every major bank that serviced Epstein filed its suspicious activity reports years late**, typically only after his 2019 arrest or death, representing what Senator Ron Wyden has called a systemic failure of the Bank Secrecy Act.

**JPMorgan** maintained 134 accounts for Epstein from 1998 to 2013, filing no SARs between 2003 and 2008 during peak trafficking activity. After Epstein's death, two retroactive SARs filed in September 2019 covered approximately **4,700 transactions totaling $1.1–1.3 billion** — roughly 300 times larger than pre-arrest filings. Flagged individuals included Leon Black, Glenn Dubin, Alan Dershowitz, and Leslie Wexner trusts (~$65M in wire transfers). Two accounts were linked to Russian banks Alfa Bank and Sberbank. Top executive Mary Erdoes was in "constant contact" with Epstein, and executive John Duffy counseled Epstein on evading cash reporting requirements. JPMorgan settled with victims for $290 million in 2023.

**Deutsche Bank** opened 40+ accounts from August 2013 (immediately after the JPMorgan relationship ended) through mid-2019, classifying Epstein as "high-risk" from onboarding but maintaining the relationship for five years. Attorney Darren Indyke made **97 cash withdrawals of exactly $7,500 each** — the bank's limit for third-party withdrawals — occurring 2–3 times monthly from 2013 to 2017, constituting textbook structuring. In one documented instance, Indyke asked about the $10,000 reporting threshold and, upon learning it would trigger a report, split the withdrawal over two days. NYDFS fined Deutsche Bank $150 million in July 2020.

**Bank of New York Mellon** moved **$378 million through 270 wire transfers** without identifying a legitimate business purpose, including at least 18 round-dollar wire transfers in $1 million installments in 2007. BNY failed to flag transactions with Treasury until 2019.

**Bank of America** filed two SARs in 2020 covering **$170 million** in transactions between Epstein and Leon Black — the Senate Finance Committee discovered the true amount exceeded the $158M identified by Apollo's board investigation by an additional $12M in previously unknown transfers. Trial is set for May 11, 2026.

The Treasury Department's "Epstein File" documents **4,725 wire transfers totaling approximately $1.1 billion** from a single account, with additional hundreds of millions flowing through other accounts. The file shows Epstein used **multiple Russian banks now under sanctions** to process payments correlated with the movement of women and girls from Russia, Belarus, Turkey, and Turkmenistan. The Trump administration has refused to produce this file to the Senate; Senator Wyden introduced the "Produce Epstein Treasury Records Act" (S.2746) to compel disclosure.

The corporate structure enabling these flows centered on **Southern Trust Company Inc.**, incorporated in the USVI in 2011 with $391.3 million in assets by 2015, reporting ~$184 million in revenue from a single unidentified source between 2013 and 2017 while claiming to provide "database/informatics/datamining" services to justify $73.6 million in tax exemptions the USVI government later alleged were fraudulent. Aviation shell companies — Plan D LLC (holding the Boeing 727 "Lolita Express" and Gulfstream G550), Hyperion Air LLC (helicopters shuttling passengers to Little St. James) — underwent serial renaming (Air Ghislaine → Shmitka Air → Freedom Air International → Hyperion Air) to obscure ownership.

The **$25 million Edmond de Rothschild agreement**, signed October 5, 2015, paid Epstein's Southern Trust Company for "risk analysis" and "application of certain algorithms" — with payment triggered when the Rothschild Group completed a **$45.2 million penalty** under the DOJ's Swiss Bank Program for helping U.S. clients hide undeclared assets. Wires totaling $25 million hit Epstein's accounts on December 17 and 21, 2015. Emails show approximately 50 meetings between Epstein and Ariane de Rothschild from 2013 to 2019.

## Focus Area 3: Victim testimony reveals both documented patterns and critical gaps

The NPR investigation published **today, February 24, 2026**, represents the most significant new development in victim testimony corroboration, identifying two accusers whose FBI interview records have been withheld or removed from the public database.

The first accuser, from approximately 1983, alleged that at age 13, Epstein introduced her to Trump, "who subsequently forced her head down to his exposed penis which she subsequently bit. In response, Trump punched her in the head and kicked her out." The FBI's National Threat Operations Center forwarded this tip to the Washington Field Office for a formal interview, and the bureau interviewed this woman **four times**. Of 15 documents listed in Maxwell case discovery logs for this accuser, only 7 are publicly available. NPR identified **53 pages of interview documents and notes missing** by cross-referencing three sets of serial numbers.

The second accuser — "Jane," the Interlochen victim — testified at Ghislaine Maxwell's 2021 trial that Epstein recruited her at age 13 from the Interlochen Center for the Arts in 1994, and later brought her to Mar-a-Lago where he introduced her to Trump, saying **"This is a good one, huh"** while both men chuckled. Her FBI interview describing this encounter was removed from DOJ files after the January 30, 2026 publication and republished February 19. Her mother's interview — recalling hearing that "a prince and DONALD TRUMP visited EPSTEIN's house" — remains offline.

**Virginia Giuffre**, the most extensively documented victim, was recruited by Maxwell at age 16 while working as a spa attendant at Mar-a-Lago in summer 2000. She described being trafficked to Prince Andrew (three occasions), Jean-Luc Brunel, Glenn Dubin, Marvin Minsky, Bill Richardson, and others. Regarding Trump, she said in a 2016 deposition that she met him at Mar-a-Lago but could not recall seeing him at Epstein's homes; her co-author confirmed Giuffre said Trump "was not involved in the ring." Giuffre died by suicide in April 2025 at age 41, in Neergabby, Australia. Her death prompted her father to publicly reject the suicide conclusion.

Corroborating evidence for victim accounts includes flight logs (48 documented entries for Giuffre alone), the authenticated photo of Giuffre with Prince Andrew and Maxwell at Maxwell's London townhouse (developed March 13, 2001 at a Florida Walgreens), Epstein's $200,000 donation to Interlochen establishing the "Epstein Scholarship Lodge," and testimony from multiple household staff including butler Rinaldo Rizzo (who witnessed a 15-year-old Swedish girl crying after Maxwell attempted to force her to have sex with Epstein) and pilot David Rodgers.

Two items — the **"Baby Stuff" thread** and the **"JE Angels" emails to Karyna Shuliak** — could not be located in publicly indexed sources and likely reside within the raw DOJ email troves accessible through JMail.world or direct file analysis. The Michael Capponi shuttle bus allegation remains an **unverified anonymous FBI tip** with no corroboration, though a separately documented 1992/1993 "calendar girl competition" at Mar-a-Lago organized by George Houraney — where Trump and Epstein were the only two male VIP guests among approximately 28 young women — provides contextual overlap.

## Focus Area 4: MCC evidence reveals a cascade of failures and contested forensics

The DOJ released **419 MCC surveillance recordings** totaling approximately 412.5 hours across five camera positions in the 9 South Special Housing Unit. Community analysis of file EFTA00028842 shows camera C-B14, labeled "J Epstein," capturing a figure in orange prison clothing on the cell floor at **4:29 AM on August 10, 2019** — roughly two hours before the body was officially discovered.

The critical forensic finding concerns the footage itself. Analysis by Garrett Discovery (for Inside Edition) and UC Berkeley's Hany Farid (for WIRED) found the released video was **processed through Adobe Premiere Pro** — not raw DVR output — with cursor and menu artifacts indicating it was a screen recording. A one-minute gap appears from 11:58:59 PM August 9 to midnight August 10, with changes in aspect ratio and screen alignment post-gap. The FBI later acknowledged possessing a complete version without this gap. Most critically, the FBI **destroyed its master copy** of surveillance video in June 2024 after an agent obtained authorization, describing it as "no longer pertinent" — before the Transparency Act mandated disclosure.

The August 8–10 timeline reveals compounding failures. On August 8, Epstein signed the 1953 Trust (estate valued at **$577.6 million**), and MCC staff first detected that DVR 2 had stopped recording on July 29 — meaning three of four cameras covering Epstein's tier were not functioning. Replacement hard drives were obtained August 9 but never installed. That same day, Epstein's cellmate was transferred with no replacement assigned despite a written directive requiring one, and the Second Circuit unsealed ~2,000 pages of Maxwell litigation documents. On the evening of August 9, Epstein made an **unrecorded, unmonitored phone call**, telling staff he was calling his mother — who had died in 2004 — actually calling girlfriend Karyna Shuliak for approximately 15 minutes.

At approximately 10:39 PM on August 9, surveillance footage shows an **unidentified orange-colored figure ascending the stairway** toward Epstein's locked tier. The FBI described it as "possibly an inmate"; officials suggested it was a correctional officer carrying orange linen. Officer Tova Noel stated: "I never gave out linen. Ever." CBS News consulted forensic video experts who assessed the movement as more consistent with a person in prison uniform. The figure's identity has never been determined.

The autopsy, performed August 11 by **Dr. Kristin Roman** (then Deputy Chief Medical Examiner), documented fractures of both superior horns of the thyroid cartilage and the left hyoid bone — each with hemorrhage. Dr. Roman initially listed the manner of death as **"pending"** rather than suicide. Mark Epstein stated she told the family the injuries "looked too much like a homicide." Chief Medical Examiner Dr. Barbara Sampson upgraded the ruling to suicide on August 16 after reviewing undisclosed "additional investigative information." Dr. Michael Baden, present at the autopsy, stated that in 50+ years and 1,000+ jail hangings, he had **never seen three fractures** in a suicidal hanging.

Among the DOJ's released files, at least **two draft press statements are dated August 9** — one day before Epstein was officially found dead — containing language about his death being "confirmed" that morning. The DOJ called this an "unfortunate typo," noting the day of the week (Friday) was correct. Separately, a 2020 DOJ email from an AUSA in the Eastern District of New York referred to an **"investigation into the murder of Jeffrey Epstein"** — notable given the official suicide ruling.

The 4chan evidence trail shows two posters with accurate information before media reports: one at 8:16 AM EDT on August 10 (38–45 minutes before first press reports) providing medically precise details, and another at 8:44 AM claiming to be a prison guard. FBI subpoenas to 4chan yielded IP addresses that led to an MCC guard named Roberto Grijalva (currently a deputy captain at MDC), but the investigation reached a dead end due to dynamic IP allocation. The NYT vs. Bureau of Prisons FOIA litigation drove early disclosures before the Transparency Act, producing the suicide watch chronological log, post-mortem review, and internal emails showing BOP-SDNY friction on the day of death.

## Conclusion: the architecture of selective disclosure

The Epstein OSINT ecosystem — spanning the Notion database, 78+ community platforms, and three major GitHub repositories — has enabled a distributed, open-source investigation of unprecedented scale. What emerges from this sweep is not a single narrative but a documented pattern of **institutional failures that consistently favor opacity**: banks filing SARs years late covering $1.5+ billion in suspicious transactions; the FBI destroying its master surveillance copy; the DOJ removing files mentioning Trump and leaving 53 pages of FBI interviews unaccounted for; and forensic evidence (Adobe-processed video, pre-dated press statements, an unidentified figure on the stairs) that contradicts official explanations without definitively proving alternatives.

The most time-sensitive finding is NPR's February 24, 2026 investigation, which provides the first methodical accounting of withheld FBI interview records — establishing through serial number analysis that specific documents about Trump accusations exist in DOJ inventories but have not been made public despite the Transparency Act's mandate. The financial evidence is equally significant: the Treasury Department's "Epstein File" documenting $1.1 billion in wire transfers through sanctioned Russian banks remains unproduced under the current administration. The community OSINT infrastructure — particularly the rhowardstone knowledge graph (524 entities, 2,096 relationships) and the Notion database's structured entity profiles — provides the architecture for continued analysis, but the most consequential information appears to reside in the gap between what the DOJ has catalogued and what it has actually released.
