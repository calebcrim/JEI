**Critical note on NTOC reliability:** Per the site's existing verification methodology, FBI NTOC Guardian complaints are at the lower end of the reliability spectrum. Many NTOC tips are flagged "not credible" by agents themselves. However, the FBI FD-302 interview (EFTA01249207) is a formal sworn interview conducted by Special Agents and is in the moderate-reliability tier. The database query result — FBI internal Sentinel/Guardian returning 26 hits on Lutnick including a money laundering case and a RICO case — is independently significant because it documents that the FBI already had existing open/closed investigations involving Lutnick at the time of the Epstein filing.

**Additionally noted from EFTA00263107 (Wikipedia):** "Wexner purchased in 1988 the adjacent townhouse at 11 East 71st Street. Like in the case of the 9 East 71st Street house, Epstein was on the deed of the 11 East 71st Street house as the trustee. The townhouse was sold in 1996 to the Comet trust which holds part of the assets of the de Gunzburg/Bronfman [family]."

**RECONCILIATION NOTE:** Wikipedia identifies the Comet Trust as holding "de Gunzburg/Bronfman" assets; the FBI FD-302 whistleblower identifies Lutnick as the buyer "through a trust." These may not be contradictory (Lutnick could have purchased from the Comet Trust in a subsequent transaction), but the ACRIS probe is still required to resolve the full deed chain. **Do not implement Lutnick as the VERIFIED deed holder at 11 E 71st until the ACRIS pull is done.** The FBI FD-302 language should be implemented as a documented FBI interview claim with appropriate verification status.
