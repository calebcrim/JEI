### FINDING 1 — "Body Boxes" Phrase is a Product Name, NOT Trafficking Terminology

**VERIFIED via primary EFTA documents (Bates-stamped)**

The phrase "body boxes" that community researchers flagged as suspicious is fully explained by two EFTA documents:

| Bates Number | Date | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **EFTA00764733** | Feb 10, 2010 | Keith Frankel → Jeffrey Epstein. Subject: **"FW: The Body Boxes"**. Body: "SO- NU?" with attachment `The_BODY_Box.pdf` |
| **EFTA00764728** | Feb 10–11, 2010 | Multi-message chain. Frankel pitches Epstein on **"THE BODY — 28 DAY BODY SCULPTING SYSTEM"** — a health/supplement product line modeled on QuickTrim. Epstein: "don't waste your time with the box. ingredients. concepts. box design and girls would be vs." Frankel: "THIS IS THE PRODUCT SUGGESTION: LET ME DO WHAT I KNOW HOW TO DO... CORE ITEM DESIGNED AROUND CLEANSING AND BURNING LIKE QUICK TRIM." |

**Conclusion:** "The Body Boxes" refers to **product packaging for a body sculpting supplement line** being pitched to Victoria's Secret. The phrase has no ambiguous or sinister meaning in context.

**Also relevant — EFTA02683530 (Bates: EFTA_R1_02001115–02001118):**  
A separate March 2010 email chain shows Frankel writing: "We are getting hounded, we are going to lose the deal and VS is going to end up buying from a broker. Need your help." The chain includes a forwarded email from **Shelly Marchetti, APA Talent and Literary Agency (Branded Lifestyles/Licensing, Beverly Hills)** to a redacted address: "just spoke with the lingerie company and they operate under international intimates for private label with Victoria Secrets — They are going to get a few dates in April." Epstein adds context: "this is the company who wants to talk about a deal with the girls and they say they manufacture for Victoria Secret."

This confirms the "body boxes" thread is a commercial product licensing deal involving Victoria's Secret and a talent agency — not a trafficking reference.

### FINDING 3 — Frankel Family Ukraine Children's Home = Tikva Children's Home, Odessa

**CORROBORATED via public record (NGO registration, news coverage, IRS 990)**

The NJ Jewish News article ("A Modern-Day Exodus," [njjewishnews.timesofisrael.com/a-modern-day-exodus/](https://njjewishnews.timesofisrael.com/a-modern-day-exodus/)) documents the Frankel family's connection to a children's home in Ukraine. Independent corroboration:

- A 2016 Tikva Children's Home gala press release lists **"Ed & Leah Frankel"** as sponsors
- A Sun Sentinel article (April 24, 2017) quotes **"Ed Frankel, chairman of the board of Tikva"** describing the organization's work in Odessa
- **Tikva Children's Home** is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 22-3779212, based in West Caldwell, NJ (8 Henderson Dr.)
- Founded in 1996 by Rabbi Shlomo Bakst
- Mission: care for homeless, abandoned, and abused Jewish children in Odessa, Ukraine
- Ed Frankel (chairman) states: "more than 500 children from Tikva's homes and schools in Odessa have successfully relocated to Israel"
- A 2016 Newswire press release notes the organization serves ~2,500 Jewish children in Odessa who are at risk of homelessness, trafficking, and exploitation

**Identity note:** "Ed Frankel" is the Tikva chairman. Keith Frankel's specific relationship to Ed Frankel (father, relative) is not independently confirmed in these sources but is consistent with the community researcher's claim ("father of Epstein pal runs a child charity in Ukraine").

**Conclusion:** The Frankel family Ukraine charity is **Tikva Children's Home in Odessa** — a fully documented, IRS-registered Jewish humanitarian organization. This is a legitimate NGO, not evidence of trafficking. The mission involves helping at-risk Jewish children and facilitating immigration to Israel. The NJ Jewish News article likely profiles this philanthropic work.
