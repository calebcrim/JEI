## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Phase 1: Full Read-Through (do not skip any file)

1. Read each of the 9 files **in full**, start to finish. For HTML files, extract the text content — ignore CSS/JS styling.
2. As you read, maintain a running internal index of every named person encountered. For each person, note:
   - Which source file(s) mention them
   - What role/relationship to Epstein is described
   - Any specific claims, dates, documents, financial figures, legal actions, quotes, or EFTA numbers tied to them

### Phase 2: Categorize All Individuals

Group every named individual into one of the following categories. If a person fits multiple categories, place them in the **primary** category and cross-reference the others:

1. **Epstein's Inner Circle** — Staff, attorneys, financial managers, household employees (e.g., Lesley Groff, Darren Indyke, Richard Kahn, Kellen, Marcinkova, Alfredo Rodriguez, Juan Alessi, etc.)
2. **Co-Conspirators & Immunity Grantees** — Named in NPA or as co-conspirators in any filing (e.g., Maxwell, Brunel, Sarah Kellen, Nadia Marcinkova, Adriana Ross, etc.)
3. **Political Figures** — U.S. and international politicians, diplomats, government officials (e.g., Trump, Clinton, Acosta, Barr, Bondi, Blanche, Higgins, Frost, Wicker, Wyden, Mandelson, Jagland, Lajčák, Mette-Marit, etc.)
4. **Intelligence & Law Enforcement** — FBI agents, prosecutors, police, intelligence-linked figures (e.g., Reiter, Fisten, Villafaña, Schnitt, Comey, Berman, Strauss, Churkin, Barak/intelligence angle, etc.)
5. **Financial Network** — Bankers, investors, financial facilitators (e.g., Wexner, Leon Black, Glenn Dubin, JPMorgan executives, Deutsche Bank contacts, Jes Staley, Mary Erdoes, John Duffy, etc.)
6. **Academic & Scientific Network** — Professors, researchers, university administrators (e.g., Larry Summers, Joi Ito, Marvin Minsky, Stephen Hawking, Noam Chomsky, George Church, Martin Nowak, etc.)
7. **Media & Legal Figures** — Journalists, lawyers, legal commentators (e.g., Michael Wolff, Julie Brown, Dershowitz, Brad Edwards, David Boies, Cheney Mason, etc.)
8. **Royalty & International Elites** — (e.g., Prince Andrew, Sultan bin Sulayem, Crown Princess Mette-Marit, etc.)
9. **Victims & Accusers** — Use pseudonyms or publicly known names only as they appear in the source files (e.g., Virginia Giuffre, Courtney Wild, "Jane," "Katie Johnson," Carolyn, etc.)
10. **Other Named Individuals** — Anyone who doesn't fit above (e.g., celebrity names on flight logs, social contacts, minor mentions)

For **every** person, write a structured entry containing:

```
- EFTA document numbers referencing them

```

### Phase 4: Redundancy Rule

**The cardinal rule:** If a fact, quote, date, dollar figure, EFTA number, or detail appears in Source File A and is *not yet present* in your drafted entry from Source File B, **add it**. Only omit information that is truly 100% identical to something already written — same fact, same level of detail, same context. When in doubt, include it.

Specifically watch for:
- **Slightly different numbers** across files (e.g., "at least 7 flights" vs. "at least 8 flights" vs. "at least one flight with a 20-year-old") — include all variants and note the discrepancy
- **Different quotes from the same email** — some files excerpt different portions; include all excerpted portions
- **Unique sourcing attributions** — one file may attribute a fact to CBS News, another to NPR, another to the House Oversight Committee; note all attributions
- **Details that appear only in one source** — these are the most important to capture since they'd otherwise be lost
