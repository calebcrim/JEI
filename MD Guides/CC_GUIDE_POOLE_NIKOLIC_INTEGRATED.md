## PROBE 2 DETAIL — The Washington Post Article

The article Nikolic sent (AR2010080906102) has been fully identified and recovered:

**Title:** "4chan users seize Internet's power for mass disruptions" (print: "An online army and its hive mind")
**Author:** Ariana Eunjung Cha, Washington Post Foreign Service
**Published:** August 10, 2010
**Recovered via:** Irish Times reprint, August 13, 2010, headline "Spinning a new web"

**Article content:** Documents 4chan coordinating mass collective action at zero cost. Seven examples:
- Gaming Google Trends (coordinated flood, swastika to #1)
- False Steve Jobs heart attack story causing Apple stock drop
- JFK airport bomb hoax triggering evacuation
- Hacking Sarah Palin's email during 2008 presidential campaign
- Rigging Time magazine's "Most Influential Person" poll to make moot the winner
- Manipulating Justin Bieber tour poll (North Korea vote)
- Prompting SEC, DHS, and FBI inquiries

Poole explained the mechanism: users seize an idea, coordinate timing through boards and chat, bloggers amplify, mainstream media picks up. **Small coordinated anonymous action cascades into mass narrative shifts.**

At the time: 4chan had 11 million unique visitors, 730 million monthly page views, and 1 million new posts daily — overwhelmingly males aged 18–25. Joshua Schachter (Delicious founder, Poole investor) compared it to financial markets: "chaotic... incredibly vital to understanding how people operate together on very, very large scales."

**Why this directly explains "the potential for manipulation is huge":** The article is a case study in demonstrated market manipulation, information ecosystem gaming, cascading amplification, complete anonymity/deniability, and asymmetric cost-effectiveness (free vs. millions). Nikolic was sending it to Epstein as context for why Poole's platform was strategically interesting — not Poole as a person, but 4chan as an anonymous manipulation engine.

## PROBE 3 DETAIL — The Seattle Trip and Bill Gates

In October 2011, Boris Nikolic held the title of **Chief Advisor for Science and Technology to Bill Gates** at **bgC3 (Bill Gates Catalyst 3)** — Gates' private office and investment think tank headquartered at 4110 Carillon Point, Kirkland, Washington (Seattle metro). Nikolic joined the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation in 2007, bgC3 in 2010, and held this dual role until April 2014. Epstein himself described Nikolic in a January 2010 email as **"bill gates right hand."**

Nikolic's function: identify disruptive technologies and interesting people for Gates' investment portfolio. Per Nikolic's own explanation to Epstein (October 2017 email), bgC3 was *"where Bill does his investments, projects, and work that crosses his work for MSFT + the foundation."*

**Evidence pointing toward a Gates introduction:**
- Seattle was Nikolic's base of operations and bgC3's HQ
- His job description literally involved scouting disruptive tech figures for Gates
- The secrecy ("he does NOT know that yet... keep it confidential") is consistent with a surprise high-level meeting needing advance vetting
- Gates' orbit was already investing in platforms organizing online communities (ResearchGate received $10M from Gates in 2013)
- Poole had just raised Series A for Canvas Networks (community-driven media platform) from Marc Andreessen, Kenneth Lerer, and Joshua Schachter — making him an active fundraising target

**Conclusion: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED.** No document directly names Gates as the planned Seattle contact. Whether the trip occurred is also unconfirmed — Garbage Day's Ryan Broderick reports "nearly a hundred emails going back and forth about how Poole kept flaking on them." Poole's February 2026 statement makes no mention of Seattle.

## PROBE 4 DETAIL — Post-2012 Contact

No evidence of Epstein-Poole contact after approximately February 2012 exists in 3.5 million released EFTA pages. Independent newsroom analyses (NBC/AP/CNBC/CBS collaborative review via MS NOW/MSNBC; Garbage Day newsletter) reach the same conclusion. Poole's own statement (February 13, 2026, The Verge): *"His assistant reached out to me afterward, and I met with him one time for an unmemorable lunch meeting... **I did not meet him again nor maintain contact.**"*

Poole does not appear in Epstein flight logs or the "little black book." No financial transactions documented. Multiple researchers have searched "moot," "Chris Poole," "Christopher Poole," and "christopher_poole" — all findings match the Oct 2011–Feb 2012 window.

**Epstein's 4chan interest persisted despite losing Poole contact:** In 2017, Epstein sent girlfriend Karyna Shuliak a 4chan link with explicit content. In 2017, journalist Michael Wolff connected Epstein with Steve Bannon; they subsequently discussed using 4chan and cryptocurrency for populist/nationalist coalition-building. In 2015, Epstein intermediaries corresponded with far-right YouTuber Jean-François Gariépy, offering PR/marketing advice.
