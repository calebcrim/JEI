**The January–February 2026 EFTA document releases have triggered the most consequential phase of the Epstein case since his 2019 arrest**, producing criminal charges in three countries, at least a dozen high-profile resignations, bipartisan congressional fury over DOJ withholding, and an emerging open-source forensic research ecosystem that in some respects outpaces official investigations. The most significant findings cluster around three axes: verified financial architecture totaling **$2.146 billion** across eight shell entities, systematic DOJ document suppression affecting **90+ FBI interview records**, and a concentrated Norwegian diplomatic network now facing criminal prosecution.

## Forensic finance reveals $2.1B flowing through eight shell entities

The GitHub repository **randallscott25-star/epstein-forensic-finance**, published February 2026, represents the most systematic computational forensic accounting analysis of the EFTA corpus to date. Built by Randall Scott Taylor, a self-described finance professional applying multi-affiliate reconciliation methods, the project processes **10,964 unique transactions** across **39 relational database tables** containing **26.6 million rows**.

The financial architecture maps four tiers. At the banking level, **Deutsche Bank** processed **$851.9M**, JPMorgan Chase **$670.8M**, and Bank of America **$486.4M** (all unverified aggregate figures). Below the banks sit eight shell entities with gross flows of $4.37B ($2.146B net), the largest being Southern Trust Company ($692M inflows), Haze Trust ($618M inflows), and Southern Financial LLC ($606.9M inflows). Southern Financial LLC shows a **$412.3M net positive position** — an unexplained surplus the author flags as a major open question. Eight inter-shell transfers totaling **$260.4M** circulated funds internally with no visible external economic purpose.

The operator tier identifies **Darren Indyke** (Epstein's attorney) processing $320.1M, **Eileen Alexanderson** at $294M with a $285M gap between outflows and documented inflows, and **Lyle Casriel** who routed **$92.5M to Ghislaine Maxwell** across 176 payments. At the terminal node level, **Leon Black** shows **$310.5M verified across 42 wire transfers** from five Black-controlled entities (Black Family Partners LP, BV70 LLC, Narrow Holdings LLC, Elysium Management), backed by specific Bates-stamped bank documents including DB-SDNY-0002962 through DB-SDNY-0006113 and EFTA01075607. A single October 2008 wire from Epstein to Black Family Partners LP transferred **$50M** (EFTA01075607).

The project's most intellectually honest contribution is its "Verification Wall" (Narrative 20), which reveals that **94.8% of NLP-extracted fund flow records are low-confidence noise**. Celebrity names produce zero verified bank documents: Donald Trump ($64.7M claimed, **$0 verified**), Bill Gates ($36.6M claimed, **$0 verified**), Bill Clinton ($17.6M claimed, **$0 verified**), Prince Andrew ($13.9M claimed, **$0 verified**). These phantom transactions derive from news clippings in forwarded emails where names appear near dollar amounts. The standard established — "Bates stamp or it didn't happen" — separates this project from less rigorous OSINT efforts.

**Reliability**: Moderate-high for bank-document-verified data; low for unverified NLP extractions. The self-correction and transparency about limitations is unusually rigorous for open-source work.

## DOJ withheld 90+ FBI interviews, triggering constitutional crisis

The most consequential finding across all sources is the documented pattern of DOJ document suppression. Three independent analyses converge on the same conclusion: the Department of Justice has withheld significant numbers of FBI FD-302 interview records from the EFTA release.

**NPR's Stephen Fowler** (published February 24, 2026) identified **53 pages** of missing FBI interviews and notes, specifically related to a woman who accused Trump of sexual abuse circa 1983 when she was approximately 13. The FBI interviewed this accuser four times; only the first interview (July 24, 2019, EFTA01245620) appears in the public database — and it does not mention Trump. Of 15 documents listed in Maxwell discovery material for this accuser, only 7 are public. **CNN independently confirmed** that a Maxwell evidence log lists serial numbers for approximately **325 FBI witness interview records**, of which **over 90 (more than 25%)** are missing from the DOJ website.

A separate researcher claims to have identified **151 unreleased FD-302 documents** from case 31E-NY-3027571 (later 50D-NY-3027571) by cross-referencing serial numbers 1–700+ in EFTA01684300.pdf against publicly released documents. The methodology — searching "3027571 Serial" to get ~400 hits, then individually checking each missing serial — is consistent with the approaches used by NPR and CNN but appears to cover a broader scope. No public coverage of this specific 151-document claim was found, though both FBI case numbers are confirmed authentic. The website case50d.com catalogs **617 FBI documents, 88 investigative stories, and 93 identified victims** from the primary case file, confirming six overlapping FBI case files including 50D-NY-3027571 (SDNY Child Sex Trafficking), 31E-MM-108062 (Miami/Palm Beach), 72-MM-113327 (Obstruction of Justice), and 90A-NY-3151227 (Death Investigation).

The political consequences have been immediate. At the **February 11, 2026 House Judiciary Committee hearing**, AG Pam Bondi told Rep. Ted Lieu: "There is no evidence that Donald Trump has committed a crime." When Rep. Dan Goldman asked Bondi to provide the **86-page SDNY prosecution memo** ("Investigation into Potential Co-Conspirators," sent to US Attorney Geoffrey Berman December 19, 2019) and a **draft Florida indictment** against co-conspirators, Bondi refused. Rep. Robert Garcia, having independently reviewed unredacted files at DOJ, stated the only remaining explanation under EFTA exemption categories is that "President Trump is under a federal investigation." Reps. Lieu and Goldman subsequently demanded a special counsel investigation of Bondi for potential perjury.

Key EFTA document numbers for this thread: EFTA01660651 (FBI allegations involving Trump), EFTA01660622 (internal "prominent names" PowerPoint), EFTA00095751 (non-testifying witness material list), EFTA00158473 (removed and restored Trump-related interview), EFTA01245486 (accuser's mother interview, still offline).

**Reliability**: High. Corroborated by NPR, CNN, bipartisan congressional members, and the Washington Post, which reported February 26 that DOJ is "reviewing" whether Trump-related files were wrongly withheld.

Rød-Larsen and his wife **Mona Juul** (also a diplomat) have both been charged. Rød-Larsen's IPI allegedly brought in young, unqualified women from Eastern Europe on short internships — a pattern that parallels trafficking methodologies documented elsewhere in the Epstein network.

The Rød-Larsen connection extends into pandemic planning. A **March 19, 2015 email** from **Walter Kemp** (IPI Director for Europe, first recipient of the Epstein-funded Rick Hooper Distinguished Fellowship) to Rød-Larsen — forwarded to Epstein the next day — attached a draft agenda titled "Preparing for Pandemics" and proposed co-branding with WHO and ICRC. Experts note pandemic preparedness discussions were standard post-Ebola. The IBTimes UK article framing this story sits alongside debunked claims about Epstein and Minecraft, significantly diminishing its interpretive credibility even as the underlying document is authentic.

**Reliability**: Very high for the Brende/WEF facts (AP, Bloomberg, CNN, Al Jazeera corroboration). High for the Norwegian criminal charges. Mixed for the pandemic planning interpretation — the document is real, the conspiratorial framing is not supported.

## Three research databases offer complementary forensic capabilities

The open-source research ecosystem has developed three distinct tools with non-overlapping capabilities:

**justice.geeken.dev** (Epstein Files Explorer) tracks DOJ deletions and modifications using cryptographic verification (SHA256 hashes) and HTTP metadata (ETags, Last-Modified headers). It identified files believed never uploaded, including EFTA00709804–00709807, EFTA00770595, EFTA00774768, and others. Notably, **10.1GB of video files** were removed from the DOJ website, including EFTA00276494.ts (4.29GB) and EFTA01244748.wmv (3.90GB). The community upvoting system ranks deleted documents by perceived significance.

**tommycarstensen.com/epstein/** is the most comprehensive multimedia archive, maintained by bioinformatician Tommy Carstensen (published in Nature, PNAS, Nature Communications). It contains **313,000+ extracted images** with OpenCV facial recognition identifying **388 individuals**, video transcriptions using OpenAI Whisper large-v3 (including surveillance footage, Maxwell interviews, and a covert recording of house manager Alfredo Rodriguez), and decoded victim diaries using rail fence cipher decryption. The findings page identifies **Leon Black** as a "Human Trafficking Subject" in SDNY emails from June 2023 (EFTA02731636, EFTA00156644), documents rape accusations against **Jes Staley** in a forensically-verified victim diary (EFTA02731465), and names **Ted Leonsis** and AOL executives including Steve Case and Jim Kimsey.

**epsteinalysis.com** indexes **1.05 million documents** and **2.08 million pages** with unique analytical capabilities including **MinHash LSH** (Locality-Sensitive Hashing) for redaction inconsistency detection — identifying cases where the same document appears with different redactions across releases, revealing what the government attempted to conceal. It also provides network visualization and structured event/meeting extraction.

## Content suppression occurs primarily at government level

The research found that the most significant content suppression is occurring through DOJ document management rather than social media censorship. Documents were temporarily removed from the DOJ website after initial January 30 publication; EFTA00158473 (an interview mentioning Trump) was removed and only restored February 19 after public outcry. An interview with an accuser's mother (EFTA01245486) mentioning "a prince and DONALD TRUMP" remains offline. Botched redactions allowed names to be recovered through copy-paste, Ctrl+F, or PDF conversion, while Base64-encoded email attachments contained fully unredacted documents.

On Reddit, **"The Overlords: Who Was Jeffrey Epstein Really"** post (r/Epstein, ID 1rfy0tj) could not be retrieved through any search method, suggesting it has been deleted or removed. The anonymous developer of "Epstein Studio" voluntarily took down the collaborative research tool within hours of it going viral after a Mario Nawfal tweet, citing legal liability and personal safety concerns. TikTok censorship claims about the word "Epstein" being blocked in DMs were investigated by eight academics and **found to have no evidence** of targeted suppression.

**Susan Hamblin** was identified through partially visible redactions as the sender of a June 30, 2014 email to Epstein reading: "I give you permission to kill him… He lied to both of us." She also sent the "your littlest girl was a little naughty" email following a David Heyman celebration. Hamblin successfully sued The Sun in UK High Court in 2022 (via Carter-Ruck solicitors), securing retraction and compensation for articles linking her to Epstein-related allegations. ## Conclusion

This 14-source sweep reveals an investigative landscape where open-source researchers are systematically outperforming official transparency efforts. The forensic finance repository's "Bates stamp or it didn't happen" standard, the serial number gap analysis exposing 90+ missing FBI interviews, and the three complementary research databases collectively create an accountability infrastructure that did not exist before the EFTA release. The most concerning finding is not any single revelation but the pattern: the DOJ simultaneously released millions of pages while systematically withholding specific FBI interviews related to the sitting president, temporarily removing documents that mentioned Trump, and failing to redact victim information while redacting co-conspirator names. Whether this constitutes a cover-up or legitimate exemption under ongoing investigation provisions may prove the defining legal question of the EFTA's implementation. The Norwegian prosecution cluster — three charged, one resigned, one apologized — demonstrates that other nations are moving faster than the United States on criminal accountability, despite the underlying evidence originating from American investigative files.
