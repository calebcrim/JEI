## PART 1: "BATMAN" — IDENTITY AND NETWORK

### Identity: Ted Waitt (Gateway Computers founder)

The alias **"Batman"** used throughout the EFTA email datasets refers to **Theodore "Ted" Waitt**, billionaire philanthropist and founder of Gateway Inc. (the personal computer company). Identity is confirmed by:

- October 12, 2004 email in which **Epstein explicitly refers to Tom/Ted Waitt as "batman"**: [EFTA02333073](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%2011/EFTA02333073.pdf)

> **Note on name:** The document researcher notes "Tom Waitt" but Maxwell's legal filings confirm the correct name is **Theodore "Ted" Waitt**. Maxwell's attorneys described Waitt as a "committed partner" with whom Maxwell began a "seven-year romantic relationship" in 2003. Filing note: "In 2003, Ms. Maxwell began a seven-year romantic relationship with Theodore 'Ted' Waitt and developed a strong and loving bond with his four children, ranging in age from six to twelve." Maxwell reportedly attended Chelsea Clinton's 2010 wedding as Waitt's guest and later helped him obtain and renovate the luxury yacht *Plan B*.

### Waitt–Maxwell–Epstein Triangle

Ted Waitt was Ghislaine Maxwell's long-term romantic partner for approximately 2003–2010 — the period during which the Epstein trafficking operation was at its most active. This creates a significant structural connection:

- Waitt is connected to Maxwell as her partner
- Waitt is connected to Epstein as "Batman" — appearing as a named financial benefactor in victim correspondence
- Waitt is the founder of the **Waitt Foundation**, a philanthropic organization

The July 31, 2016 email establishes Waitt in the role of financial provider to a young woman in Epstein's orbit:

> Epstein receives email in which the sender describes telling him "how much Batman gave me per month." Epstein responds that Batman "wants to keep you short so that you can never be independent."

This framing — a wealthy man providing monthly payments to a young woman while Epstein manages the relationship and counsels her against financial independence — is consistent with the documented Epstein pattern of creating financial dependency.

## PART 2: MÉLUSINE RUSPOLI — PROBABLE IDENTITY OF CORRESPONDENT

### Background

The young woman correspondent in the August 2015 email cluster is **almost certainly Mélusine Ruspoli** (full name: Donna Mathilda Mélusine dei Principi Ruspoli, born 1994, Paris).

**Identity indicators from the emails:**
- References to attending **Marangoni** (fashion/design school) and applying to the **American University of Paris (AUP)**
- References to **Bulgari and Ferragamo internships** (Ruspoli is documented as having modeled for Ferragamo and attended Le Bal 2013 as a Chanel debutante)
- References to stepbrothers, brothers, and **Olimpia** (likely a family member)
- References to **Italy** and Italian social circles (Tancredi, Margot/Margherita)
- Italian date formats and need for TOEFL tutoring suggesting non-native English
- Use of "principessa"-level social access consistent with Roman Black Nobility background
- Father died when she was ~10 (2005) — consistent with Dado Ruspoli's death
- The application essay ghost-written by Epstein (or written in her voice with his intervention) explicitly mentions: French Lycée in Rome, French Baccalauréat, Italian/French fluency, Bulgari and Ferragamo internships, AUP application — all matching Ruspoli's documented biography
- Closing line of the application essay: **"I also will learn to give pleasure to men so I am able to support my studies"** — almost certainly Epstein's insertion, consistent with his documented grooming methodology

### The "Margot" Figure

"Margot" in the emails is inferred to be **Margherita Campello Della Spina**, connected to the Agnelli/Elkann family (Italian Fiat dynasty). "Tancredi" (whose surname is not given) is a mutual connection who was Margot's romantic interest and with whom the correspondent shared a bed — the source of the conflict with Epstein.

### The Financial Dependency Dynamic

Mélusine Ruspoli's family background explains Epstein's leverage:
- Father **Alessandro "Dado" Ruspoli** (9th Prince of Cerveteri) famously depleted the family fortune before his 2005 death — selling art, heirlooms, and family patrimony
- The family retains the title and historic property (Castello Ruspoli with its famous hedge maze) but very little liquid capital
- In 2015, Mélusine was approximately 21 years old and AUP tuition was €15,000–20,000/year
- Epstein provided education funding, monthly payments, and "guidance" — his documented grooming playbook applied to cash-poor young women from prestigious families

### The August 2015 Email Cluster — Summary

The blowup centers on the correspondent sleeping in the same bed as "Tancredi" while staying as Margot's house guest. Key dynamics:

- Epstein is enraged, frames it as betrayal, lack of moral compass, and pattern of lying
- Correspondent admits lying to Margot and others at Tancredi's request ("He made me promise not to say anything to Margo so that she wouldn't get jealous")
- She throws back at Epstein: "I could have said the truth about you to the people who paid my school, **batman** and my family but I didn't"
- Epstein had previously said he refused to get involved in a lawsuit at Trump's request (see Part 3 below for parallel dynamic)
- She quotes Epstein's own words: "Your habit and mine are very used to lie to everyone" — confirming mutual deception as a normalized operating mode
- Epstein threatens to cut her off, compares her to "the Bulgaris" whose resources she allegedly "wasted after saying you were serious"
- The AUP application essay appears to have been written or co-written by Epstein to facilitate her school placement

### Batman Reference in This Thread

The correspondent's statement that she could have told "batman" the truth about Epstein — but didn't — is significant. It establishes that Ted Waitt (Batman) was:
1. Aware of (or adjacent to) Epstein's involvement with this young woman
2. Considered a potential source of leverage against Epstein
3. Someone whose opinion/knowledge Epstein had reason to fear

This links the Waitt financial pipeline to Epstein's victim/beneficiary network.
